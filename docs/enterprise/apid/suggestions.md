# Suggestions

Fixes and next steps that APID recommends.

---

### Overview

Suggestions lists the improvements APID recommends, most important first. The summary at the top counts the open suggestions, the high impact ones, the suggestions by type, and those that are done or dismissed.

| Type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Security** | Fixes for security weaknesses |
| **Coverage** | Steps to bring more of your APIs under discovery |
| **Hygiene** | Clean-up and best-practice improvements |

![Suggestions](/img/apid/suggestions.png)

### How to Use:

1. Go to **Stack** > **APID** > **Suggestions**.
2. Search, or filter by **Open**, **Done**, **Dismissed** or by type.

Each suggestion shows its type, impact, effort, collection, status and when it was raised.
