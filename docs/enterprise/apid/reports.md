# Reports

Download the reports of your vulnerability scans.

---

### Overview

Reports lists the PDF reports of the vulnerability scans that completed successfully, for every endpoint of the stack. Scans are configured in the [DAST Scan](./endpoints/dast_scan.md) settings of an endpoint.

![Reports](/img/apid/reports.png)

### How to Use:

1. Go to **Stack** > **APID** > **Reports**.
2. Find the report by its target.
3. Click the download icon at the end of the row.

Each report shows the name of the report, the scanned target and when it was generated.
